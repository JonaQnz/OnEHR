Category: feature

## What I was trying to do

Get an overview of published forms available for a new patient by calling
`list_forms` (no arguments - it takes none).

## What happened

`list_forms` returned every form/Composition across all versions and
statuses (published, draft, archived, deleted) in one unfiltered response -
40 rows totalling 1,383,663 characters / 22,598 lines, each including the
full `canonical_json` (layout, bindings, formScript source, compiled JS,
source maps, generated TypeScript typings, etc.). The MCP call itself
errored out ("exceeds maximum allowed tokens") and I had to fall back to
reading the raw dumped file with `jq` outside the tool to get a usable
summary (id/name/version/status/kind) of just the published forms I
actually needed.

## What I expected

For routine "what forms can I use for this patient" browsing (the most
common reason to call this tool), I'd expect either:

- `list_forms` to accept filters (e.g. `status`, `kind`, or a
  "latestPublishedOnly" flag) so a normal call doesn't have to transfer
  every draft/archived/deleted version's full compiled formScript and
  sourcemaps, or
- a lighter-weight summary shape by default (id, parent_id, name, version,
  status, kind) with the full `canonical_json` only returned by `get_form`
  for a specific id (which already exists and is the right place for the
  heavy payload).

As it stands, `list_forms` is unusable for its most basic purpose ("show me
what forms exist") once a project has accumulated more than a handful of
form versions - it hits the token ceiling and forces a manual workaround
just to browse the catalog.
