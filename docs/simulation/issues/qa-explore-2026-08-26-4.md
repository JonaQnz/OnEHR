Category: bug

## What I was trying to do

Submitted the "Patientenaufnahme" form (bound to `vg_Person.v1.1.1`,
containing the patient's structured name/address/etc.) for a new patient,
then checked `get_patient` to confirm the patient record reflects that
admin/demographic data now exists in EHRbase.

## What happened

Immediately after a successful `submit_form_session_to_provider` for the
Person composition (EHRbase confirmed `lifecycleState: "complete"`,
`changeTypeConfirmed: true`), `get_patient` for the same patient still
showed:

```json
{ "hasPersonArchetype": false, "updatedAt": "...T06:19:59.544Z" }
```

- unchanged since the patient was first registered, well before the
Person composition was submitted (`06:20:25`). The flag only flipped to
`true` after I separately called `sync_patients`, which re-pulled every
patient in the registry from EHRbase.

## What I expected

`hasPersonArchetype` (or whatever downstream UI reads it to decide "does
this patient have registered admin data yet") should reflect reality right
after a successful submit of the form that creates that exact archetype,
without requiring a separate, whole-registry `sync_patients` call. As built,
a clinician who just finished admitting a patient and glances at the
patient list/header would see stale "no person data" state until someone
thinks to trigger a sync - easy to miss, and confusing since the submit
itself reported success.
